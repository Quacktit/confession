Privacy Policy

unspokenadc is built for students of Ahmedabad Dental College. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email, stored only as a one-way hash, and your password, stored only as a salted hash. We cannot read either.
  • The confessions and drafts you write.

Anonymity

There are no usernames or profiles. Other users never see who wrote a confession. Your posts are linked to your account internally only so you can see them under "My Confessions".

What we do not do

  • No likes, comments or public interactions.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no ads or trackers.

Read counts

"Popular" is based on how many times a confession was opened by other readers.

Community guidelines

Do not name or target individuals, share private details of others, or post threats. If you are struggling, use the helpline on the Confessions page.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: add your contact email here.
