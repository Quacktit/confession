Privacy Policy

unspokenadc is built for students of Ahmedabad Dental College. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

  • The email address you sign up with (stored encrypted) and a password (stored only as a salted hash, so we cannot read it).
  • The confessions and drafts you write, and how many times each confession is opened.

Who can see what

Other users never see who wrote a confession. There are no usernames or profiles. However, this site is not anonymous to the site owner. The owner can see the email address linked to any confession.

The owner uses this only to keep the site and its users safe, for example for threats, self-harm risk, harassment, explicit or illegal content, or legal requests. Each lookup is logged.

Moderation

Every confession is reviewed before it is published, and again after you edit it. The owner can edit, hide, reject or delete any confession at any time, including after publication. Edits are normally limited to removing names and identifying details.

Password reset emails

If you ask to reset your password, we send the reset link to your email through a third-party email service (Google).

Sharing your information

We do not sell your data and do not run ads or trackers. We may share your email and your posts with authorities, the college, or others if required by law, or if needed to prevent serious harm to you or others.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself from the My Confessions page. This removes your account, stored email and drafts. Your confessions stay on the site but are no longer linked to you. To have a confession removed, or your data erased in another way, use the contact below.

Community guidelines

Do not name or target individuals, share private details about others, post sexual or explicit content, or make threats. You are responsible for what you write.

Need help?

If you are struggling, use the helpline on the Confessions page. Tele-MANAS: 14416.
